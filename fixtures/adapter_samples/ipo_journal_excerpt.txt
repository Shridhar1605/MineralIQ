(57) Abstract :
The present invention provides a heat recovery stack system for biomass-fired boilers that improves energy efficiency, reduces emissions, and optimizes fuel utilization.
The system integrates a modified heat recovery stack designed to capture and repurpose waste heat from flue gases. This recovered heat is used for preheating feedwater
and drying biomass fuel i.e. adsorbent before combustion, thereby enhancing combustion efficiency and reducing emissions. The system further incorporates a
controlled exhaust ducting mechanism to ensure safe dispersion of flue gases while minimizing environmental impact. The invention includes an automated control unit
that dynamically adjusts operational parameters based on real-time data. The modular and adaptable design enables easy integration with various biomass boiler
systems, making it a cost-effective solution for industries seeking improved thermal efficiency and sustainability.

(57) Abstract :
The present invention relates to an eco-extraction system and process for recovering reusable pigment from coloured textile waste. The process comprises segregating
the waste by colour class, mechanically shredding it, contacting the comminuted feed with an eco-solvent, applying ultrasonic energy to assist release of embedded
colourant, separating and purifying the colourant-bearing liquor by membrane filtration, regenerating the purified colourant into a solid pigment, stabilising it, and
recovering a pigment powder. A pigment-recovery system comprising a shredder, extraction reactor, ultrasonic treatment chamber, filtration unit, precipitation tank,
drying chamber and milling unit connected as a continuous pipeline carries out the process. The recovered pigment is formulated with a stabilising agent and a
dispersing binder into an eco-friendly pigment composition for artist paints and printing inks. The arrangement recovers colourant without impairing its chromatic
properties, diverting textile waste from landfill and enabling a circular economy for textile colour.

(57) Abstract :
The present invention relates to a diagnostic computing apparatus (100) and signal-processing method for real-time polymerase chain reaction (rt-PCR) data analysis.
The apparatus ingests digitized relative fluorescence unit time-series arrays (104) from an external instrument (102), executing minimum-coordinate structural gating to
exclude truncated records and computing a common sample intersection matrix. The apparatus comprises a curve-fitting engine (114) that fits a continuous fourparameter sigmoidal model seeded via analog warm-start initializations bounded by a digital exponent clamping register (120) preventing floating-point overflow. An
extraction processor (116) derives feature parameters evaluated by a majority-voting processor (118) applying an asymmetric weighting matrix (121) to internal control
channels. A decision-mapping engine (122) assigns composite outcomes tabulated inside an electronic workbook (130) programmatically linked to isolated graphical
evidence records (128).
